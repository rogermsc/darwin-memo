% The disclosure exchange, and what the three MemoryOS findings are.
Findings against \textsc{MemoryOS} were sent to the corresponding author
before this section was written, with the code paths, the scripts, and an
offer to correct anything we had described wrongly; the reply neither
disputed a finding nor requested an embargo, and invited fixes through
the project's repository. The correspondence, redacted of addresses, is
archived in the artifact under \texttt{docs/disclosure/}. We record it as
what it is---the maintainers were given the findings in full and raised
no objection---and not as an endorsement: every claim about their system
rests on our own reading of their published source. Of the three
\textsc{MemoryOS} findings, one is a defect with a patch to submit; the
other two describe a mechanism working exactly as designed, which is why
they are reported as properties of a design rather than as
vulnerabilities.
